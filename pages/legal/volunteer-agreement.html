Volunteer Agreement

Last Updated: Feb 1, 2026

1. Introduction & Nature of Relationship

This agreement defines the relationship between ZDC (Zagazig Digital Community) and anyone accepted into its internal team as a contributor or volunteer. By accepting, you explicitly acknowledge that your participation is entirely voluntary.

  • Joining does not entail any financial obligation or compensation from ZDC toward the volunteer.
  • This agreement does not create an employment relationship, legal partnership, or binding labor contract.

2. Scope of Responsibilities & Professionalism

Your tasks are assigned according to your accepted role (content authoring, request review, project auditing, or tooling management). As a ZDC team member, you are expected to:

Perform assigned tasks diligently and meet agreed timelines whenever possible.
Respect fellow team members and students, and uphold ZDC's positive reputation.

3. System Privileges Management

You may be granted administrative privileges (Admin / Reviewer / Content Manager). You must strictly adhere to the following:

  • Use administrative privileges exclusively for assigned duties; never exploit them for personal gain.
  • Do not access, inspect, or modify any data unrelated to your direct responsibilities (such as private student records).

4. Strict Confidentiality

Trust is the foundation of our administrative team. It is strictly prohibited to:

  • Share or leak any user data or internal platform analytics to external parties.
  • Publish or screenshot internal admin workflows, system architecture, or proprietary source code.
  • This confidentiality obligation remains in effect even after your volunteer period concludes.

5. Intellectual Property

Any content, design, curriculum material, or code produced for ZDC during your volunteer term becomes an integral part of the platform. ZDC retains the right to use, maintain, and update these contributions indefinitely to serve students without financial claims.

6. Unacceptable Behavior & Termination

Severe Violations

  • • Abuse of administrative privileges.
  • • Tampering with platform or student records.
  • • Mistreatment or bullying of team members or learners.

Termination & Resignation

ZDC reserves the right to terminate volunteer membership and revoke access immediately upon proven violation or chronic negligence.

Volunteers may respectfully step down at any time, provided they notify leadership for a smooth handover.
