Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Feb 1, 2026

1. Introduction & Who We Are

Welcome to ZDC (Zagazig Digital Community). We are an independent, non-profit educational platform dedicated to Digital IC Design. We are fully committed to protecting your personal data transparently.

2. Data We Collect (And What We Don't)

Data Collected

  • • Name, Email address, and Profile Avatar.
  • • University, Faculty, Department, and Academic Year.
  • • Learning progress, quiz results, project submissions, and XP points.

Data NEVER Collected

  • • No financial or banking credentials.
  • • No third-party ad trackers.
  • • We never sell or rent your data to external parties.

3. How Do We Use Your Data?

Our use of your data is strictly limited to operating ZDC effectively in order to:

  • Create and manage your account and engineering profile.
  • Power the gamification system (Ranks, Badges, Teams, and Leaderboards).
  • Send essential operational emails (account verification and password recovery).

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted infrastructure providers to keep ZDC fast and reliable:

Supabase

Secure database storage and authentication services.

YouTube API

Streaming public educational lectures without sharing your profile data.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser storage (Local Storage & IndexedDB) strictly for operational purposes, such as keeping you signed in and preserving player preferences for faster loading. We do not use Google Analytics or advertising trackers.

6. Security & Your Rights

Security Level

We enforce Row Level Security (RLS) policies preventing unauthorized access to private records, and all passwords are encrypted automatically.

Right to Deletion

You can update your personal details anytime from account settings, or request full permanent deletion of your account by contacting administration.
