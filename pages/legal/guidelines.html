Community Guidelines

Last Updated: Feb 1, 2026

1. The Core of ZDC

ZDC (Zagazig Digital Community) is not just a course library; it is a vibrant engineering community driven by collaboration, constructive competition, and the pursuit of excellence in Digital IC Design. These guidelines represent the shared values that keep our environment welcoming and inspiring for everyone.

2. Respect & Inclusion

Mutual respect is our golden rule. Our community unites students and engineers across universities and skill levels. We expect every member to:

  • Support peers technically and constructively, and respond to questions with patience and professionalism.
  • Strictly Prohibited: Bullying, verbal abuse, mocking beginners' questions, harassment, or any form of discrimination.

3. Teamwork & Leadership

Teamwork is the backbone of ZDC. Team Leaders carry an important responsibility in mentoring and motivating their members.

For Leaders

Evaluate deliverables fairly, listen actively to your teammates, and never misuse privileges. True leadership is service, not authority.

For Members

Respect team milestones and deadlines. Communicate transparently whenever circumstances prevent you from submitting tasks on time.

4. Academic Integrity (Fair Play)

Ranks, badges, and leaderboards only carry meaning when earned through real engineering effort. We uphold integrity strictly:

  • Collaboration is encouraged; cheating is not: Discuss design approaches and concepts freely, but do not copy final project submissions or share exact quiz answer keys.
  • XP Integrity: Any attempt to exploit the platform to artificially farm XP will lead to XP resets and team suspension from the leaderboard.

5. Communication & Notifications

Use platform communication channels and notifications responsibly. Spamming team invitations or filing false reports against competitors is prohibited. Keep all interactions respectful and professional.

6. Reporting & Consequences

If you witness any behavior that violates these guidelines, report it to the administration immediately. All reports are treated with strict confidentiality.

How Does Administration Handle Violations?

  • 1 Issuing a formal warning and corrective guidance.
  • 2 Resetting XP points or revoking badges and ranks.
  • 3 Temporary suspension or permanent ban from ZDC.
