About ZDC

Zagazig Digital Community

In the world of Digital IC Design, knowledge exists everywhere—but it is often fragmented and hard to navigate.

ZDC was built to be your engineering compass. Our goal is not just to gather references, but to organize them into a clear, step-by-step curriculum that takes you from fundamentals to real silicon-ready projects.
